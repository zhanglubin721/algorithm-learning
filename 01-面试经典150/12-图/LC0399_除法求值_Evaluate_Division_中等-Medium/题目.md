# LC399 除法求值

Evaluate Division · Medium · 图

## 题意

给出变量间的除法等式和比值，对每个查询计算比值；无法确定返回-1，未知变量除以自身也无法确定。

## 约束要点

- 1 <= equations.length <= 20
- 1 <= Ai.length, Bi.length <= 5
- 0.0 < values[i] <= 20.0
- 1 <= queries.length <= 20
- 1 <= Cj.length, Dj.length <= 5

## 示例输入

示例1

```text
[["a","b"],["b","c"]]
[2.0,3.0]
[["a","c"],["b","a"],["a","e"],["a","a"],["x","x"]]
```

示例2

```text
[["a","b"],["b","c"],["bc","cd"]]
[1.5,2.5,5.0]
[["a","c"],["c","b"],["bc","cd"],["cd","bc"]]
```

示例3

```text

[0.5]
[["a","b"],["b","a"],["a","c"],["x","y"]]
```
